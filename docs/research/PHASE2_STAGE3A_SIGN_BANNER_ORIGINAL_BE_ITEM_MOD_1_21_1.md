## Actual algorithm entrypoints, alternate writers and physical versus semantic ownership

**Critical common item seam:** [StandingAndWallBlockItem](https://github.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/blob/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/item/StandingAndWallBlockItem.java) `getPlacementState` chooses standing/ceiling block or the wall block using `attachmentDirection`, `getNearestLookingDirections()`, both `getStateForPlacement` methods, `canPlace` and real `isUnobstructed` hit geometry.
