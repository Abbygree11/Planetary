## 3/4 — NeoForge/mod extensions, independent bypass and ownership

- NeoForge `Block.shouldRenderFace` includes **external neighbor face hiding** `hidesNeighborFace` + `supportsExternalFaceHiding`. [Earlier source review and exact selected ASM](PHASE2_STAGE3C11_SHAPES_OCCLUSION_SUPPORT_RENDER_CULLING_NEOFORGE_ASM_1_21_1.md) confirm the target’s path. A later [NeoForge comparative patch](https://github.com/neoforged/NeoForge/blob/3cae64530de368936d4c07497d3f0a25e2f9ffec/patches/net/minecraft/world/level/block/Block.java.patch) is **not necessarily exact 21.1.215**. 3B owns the full render visibility/ModelData/RenderType pipeline.
- [Create1.21.1 connected glass comparator](PHASE2_STAGE3C11_SHAPES_OCCLUSION_SUPPORT_RENDER_CULLING_NEOFORGE_ASM_1_21_1.md), [AE2 quartz glass comparator](PHASE2_STAGE3C11_SHAPES_OCCLUSION_SUPPORT_RENDER_CULLING_NEOFORGE_ASM_1_21_1.md) and CTM overlays own **independent face hiding/material** decisions. Prior pinned source versions provide representative evidence, but loaded compatible JARs/override ABI are **NOT** verified in 3R-2d.
- Moving contraptions, fake chunk `BlockGetter` and GPU/instanced rendering can bypass real `Level` and ordinary BakedModel/BER. New `3R-2d` work deliberately **does not** globally relax that `Level` guard or force all virtual getters into Planet world coordinates.
- Arbitrary NeoForge/custom shape callbacks may be overridden *without* `BlockState` property changes. The six original JVM owner inventory [3R-2c](PHASE3_R2C_ORIGINAL_SIX_SHAPE_OWNERS_1_21_1.tsv) covers vanilla registered methods but **cannot enumerate modded additions**. Need compatibility artifact pinning and full custom owner inventory before declaring whole Phase3 research complete.
- PHYSICAL/CANONICAL boundary: physical neighbor cells and ray/intersections untouched; locally authored block shape/material positions stay canonical until typed outer boundary; local target support and separate source/target occlusion faces each use their **own** block frame. Do not patch per-block IDs, rotate universal `Direction` or rewrite shared `BlockStateBase.Cache`.

**Additional gates:** `BlockStateBase.isCollisionShapeFullBlock`, `hasLargeCollisionShape`, `getFaceOcclusionShape`, `isFaceSturdy`, simple-vs-contextual overloads and `BlockState#hasDynamicShape` classification require the *complete* exact-target caller/registered state data map; selected tests do not constitute the universe. `SupportType` variants and `BlockGetter` default methods may be consumed by mods/foreign implementations independent of Planet’s current Level-bound adapter.
