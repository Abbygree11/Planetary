## 2/4 — Original semantic boundaries and independent NeoForge AO vs flat pipelines

Original `ModelBlockRenderer.tesselateBlock` target method body shows:
- `Minecraft.useAmbientOcclusion` combined with **`BakedModel.useAmbientOcclusion(BlockState,ModelData,RenderType):TriState`** (actual NeoForge extension method, not ordinary model boolean) and state/position-sensitive `BlockState.getLightEmission(BlockGetter,BlockPos)`.
- `BlockState.getOffset(BlockGetter,BlockPos)` and `PoseStack.translate` occur before `tesselateWithAO` / `tesselateWithoutAO` calls. Preserve physical offset owner, with local-to-world transformation exactly once.
- `tesselateWithAO` and `tesselateWithoutAO` each request per-side original `BakedModel.getQuads(BlockState,Direction,RandomSource,ModelData,RenderType)`, reseed `RandomSource`, check `List.isEmpty`, query physical neighbor `Block.shouldRenderFace` and process null-side model quads. Thus model-origin emptiness, visibility and light shading are independently observable causes of a missing-looking face.
- `Block.shouldRenderFace` explicitly checks original `skipRendering`, NeoForge `hidesNeighborFace` / `supportsExternalFaceHiding`, then `canOcclude`, two contextual `getFaceOcclusionShape` calls, `BooleanOp.ONLY_FIRST`; it has its **own original ThreadLocal 2048-entry `BlockStatePairKey` cache**. Planet's separate four-part directional cache also omits position/time, but this is not by itself an established mod-dynamic correctness bug. Dynamic occlusion, cache eligibility and overrides need actual face-result changes and loaded game checks, **not** the 3R-2j dynamic piston *collision* repro.
- `ModelBlockRenderer$AmbientOcclusionFace.calculate` uses `getLightBlock`, `getLightColor`, `getShade`, `getShadeBrightness` in its physical neighbor/corner sampling graph, independent from Phase2 BlockState orientation writer.

Exact target `LightPipelineAwareModelBlockRenderer` **extends** `ModelBlockRenderer`, overrides AO and no-AO paths and reads the `NeoForgeConfig.CLIENT.experimentalForgeLightPipelineEnabled` Boolean:
- enabled: uses thread-local `smoothLighter` or `flatLighter` and delegates to separate `render`;
- disabled: falls back to corresponding `ModelBlockRenderer` AO/flat implementation.
- `SmoothQuadLighter.computeLightingAt` on original target uses `BlockAndTintGetter.getBlockState`, `BlockState.getLightBlock`, `getShadeBrightness`, **two `getFaceOcclusionShape` calls** and `Shapes.faceShapeOccludes`, `LevelRenderer.getLightColor`, `LightTexture.sky/block` and physical `Direction.getStepX/Y/Z`. Hence correcting *face visibility* alone does not guarantee correct smooth-light shadowing at seams.
- `FlatQuadLighter.computeLightingAt` checks `BlockState.getCollisionShape` / `Block.isShapeFullBlock` and per-direction physical `LevelRenderer.getLightColor`. Different from AO neighbor-shape semantics. Do not globally rotate the Minecraft light engine or hardcode +Y assumptions across shared lighting consumers.
- `QuadLighter.setup` records `BlockAndTintGetter`, `BlockPos`, `BlockState` and a `Objects.hash`-derived optimization key, may reuse previous setup. This bytecode fact doesn't establish stale light if `reset` and worker lifecycle are correct; exact lifetime and getter identity remain independent tests.

**Exact target expanded JVM evidence**, not merely comparative NeoForge source: `LightPipelineAwareModelBlockRenderer.render` calls `BakedModel.getQuads` **2 times**, `Block.shouldRenderFace` **1** and `QuadLighter.process` **4** across its original code body. `SmoothQuadLighter.computeLightingAt` calls `getFaceOcclusionShape` **2** and `LevelRenderer.getLightColor` **1**; `FlatQuadLighter.computeLightingAt` calls `BlockState.getCollisionShape` **1** and `LevelRenderer.getLightColor` **2**. `QuadLighter.getColorFast` dispatches to original `BlockColors.getColor` **1**. Two different light/shade code paths share mod-aware quad and face culling, so a proposed fix must preserve both. Actual enabling of the experimental NeoForge option and Minecraft visual results remain unverified.
