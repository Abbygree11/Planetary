## 3. Side/channel separation and all direct or bypass writers

- **One original BlockItem per five exact BLOCK IDs**: `minecraft:decorated_pot,chiseled_bookshelf,barrel,chest,ender_chest`. Original vanilla BE belongs to `DecoratedPotBlockEntity`, `ChiseledBookShelfBlockEntity`, `BarrelBlockEntity`, `ChestBlockEntity`, `EnderChestBlockEntity`; ender contents reside instead in `PlayerEnderChestContainer`. `BlockItem.place` calls `getPlacementState`, `Level.setBlock` and then DataComponents.BLOCK_STATE, DataComponents.BLOCK_ENTITY_DATA/CONTAINER and `setPlacedBy`; alternative mod BlockItems may override context/state/BE components. Source inventory of actual five original per-item methods is `BlockItem`, but after-placement data can affect BE.
- **Direct in-place writers:** decorated pot ItemStack insert, crack/shatter direct `Level.setBlock/destroyBlock`; bookshelf book insertion/removal updates BE+6 occupied booleans; chest pairing is self-written placement and `updateShape`; opener/redstone tick and particles are not ordinary BlockItem writes. `/setblock`/`/fill`, structure palettes/template block entities, loot table, structure processors, mod event cancellation/placed block, WorldGenLevel feature, clipboard/fake Level all bypass direct BlockItem placement, and require explicit context provenance.
- **Sided inventory/transport:** `IItemHandler` NeoForge block capabilities, `WorldlyContainer.getSlotsForFace`, hopper `Direction.UP`/DOWN, comparator `getAnalogOutputSignal` are *provider-decided physical sides*, not automatically "local TOP" of Planet. A generic `Direction.UP` substitution or fake `Level.getBlockEntity` coordinate alias corrupts inventories and mod automation. If a known provider explicitly opt-ins to local-face semantics, transport input side should be resolved once at that exact **provider and caller** border; unknown provider gets original physical side and actual neighbor.
- **Render/physics:** Chest/Ender animated BER/lid and face-lock client model; barrel MODEL; bookshelf six-book geometry; decorated pot shard design and world-Y particles; hit AABB and physics must agree with per-target canonical frame but never add illegal BlockState values or rotate actual mod machine inventory sidedness.
- **Other original subclasses not counted in Stage22:** `TrappedChestBlock` inherits the chest algorithm with distinct redstone/openers, `ShulkerBoxBlock` has **17 original BLOCK IDs in one concrete Java class** and special inventory motion, and automation/Hopper/Dispenser/Dropper/Crafter are separate physical/logic owners; explicitly queued as next family, **NOT** declared source-reviewed by this stage. Sibling relationships alone do not justify a runtime blanket AbstractChestBlock change.
