**Delays matter.** Stage17 initiating randomTick may select WATER probability 0.17578125 / LAVA 0.05859375, require a dry downward tip with real source above, find a physically accessible eligible receiver and schedule `50 + (tipY - cauldronY)` ticks. Receiver's actual scheduled tick **re-queries** tip and exact source before changing block. No instant fill when a particle appears, and no reusing an old fluid snapshot. Empty cauldron accepts an eligible drip then maps WATER→water_cauldron LEVEL1 or LAVA→lava_cauldron; water layered accepts only WATER and only RAIN-instance, increments LEVEL until 3; snow layered rejects WATER, lava cauldron rejects all. Source MUD→CLAY is PointedDripstoneBlock's separate write path, not a cauldron receiver effect.

**Weather is NOT Block.randomTick.** ServerLevel obtains the WORLD-Y exposure surface, only calls handlePrecipitation in its raining/weather branch and when precipitation != NONE. Empty CauldronBlock rolls **0.05** per qualifying RAIN callback or **0.1** per qualifying SNOW callback and becomes water/snow LEVEL1. LayeredCauldronBlock rolls the same helper and increments exactly its OWN precipitation type, only when LEVEL<3. Rain does not fill the snow layered instance and snow does not fill water. No claim of 5% chance per game tick: chance is per qualifying precipitation callback. Lava block has no override for handlePrecipitation. On Planet faces, weather surface interpretation vs local sky is a **separate Phase8/world environment policy**; globally rotating ServerLevel heightmap or Biome is unsafe for non-Planet and fake worlds.

**Entity collision and comparison.** `AbstractCauldronBlock.isEntityInsideContent` compares physical entity.getY / boundingBox.maxY against physical block.getY + content height, so merely rotating static voxel SHAPE in a render adapter cannot orient water contact, extinguishing or lava harm. Layered water extinguishes and decreases fill; powder snow on fire replaces with water equivalent and consumes one level, possibly empty; lava deals heat damage. Expose an opt-in local content-plane check while retaining true physical Entity AABB and Level. Empty/layered/lava analog/comparator signals must use original levels; content-as-FluidState conversion is incorrect.

[CauldronEvents](https://github.com/RainLasea/BareBonesBrews/blob/4ea706f86df360a377a971f59a2cad035b1e8bd4/src/main/java/lasea/barebonesbrews/brewing/CauldronEvents.java) intercepts 1.21.1 block interactions, partly conditions vanilla CauldronInteraction.WATER map use on custom contents. **This is independent mod behavior, not subclass inheritance and not a generic cauldron state predicate.** A too-broad Planetary redirect/overwrite would swallow those cancellable callbacks or leak water extinguish/weather into custom fluids. The mod template requires NeoForge `[21.1.251,)`; therefore this exact version **cannot be assumed loadable on our 21.1.215**. Modded *loaded runtime* compatibility and actual mixin ordering NOT TESTED, use separate compatible version/runtime upgrade for future validation. Do not include custom BE as original vanilla census class.

Mod branching matrix: `AbstractCauldronBlock` subclass can override canReceive/receive, `LayeredCauldronBlock` override changes precipitation, Mixin may cancel base callback, event may intercept before useItemOn, entire independent modded block may implement its own storage, and another mod may expose capability rather than vanilla fluid. Therefore no unconditional dispatch hijack at Level/BlockState/FluidState; unknown mod defaults PHYSICAL unless semantics opt in. Vanilla-specialized Mixin cannot claim coverage of arbitrary modded receivers.
