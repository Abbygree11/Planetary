### NeoForge-specific safety and source caveat

The verified comparative NeoForge 1.21.1 patch [`VineBlock.java.patch`](https://github.com/neoforged/NeoForge/blob/3cae64530de368936d4c07497d3f0a25e2f9ffec/patches/net/minecraft/world/level/block/VineBlock.java.patch) changes random tick branch to consult `ServerLevel.isAreaLoaded(pos,4)` before spreading so random growth does not load unloaded chunks, and changes random-source used for the gate. **A local-gravity growth adapter must preserve this no-chunk-force-load behavior and gamerule `RULE_DO_VINES_SPREAD`**. NeoForge does **not** supply one universal replacement for `VineBlock`, `MultifaceSpreader`, bone meal and worldgen ownership at this pinned patch; callbacks remain independent and mod-subclass virtual extension is essential. The later NeoForge patch is a source comparator, not a claim that every patch hunk or callsite is identical in NeoForge21.1.215 without the exact classpath ASM proof.

## 2/4 — physical/canonical step and growth lifecycle

The source owner produces a `(source WorldPos, source frame/chart, source attachment face)`. A candidate attaches to physical neighbor with **target** frame; at seams, the source direction and target-local direction need a transported chart mapping that preserves the actual physical `BlockPos`. Importantly, `canAttachTo(BlockGetter, physicalSide, neighborPos, neighborState)` tests **neighbor's opposite physical support/collision face**; it is not the same as rotating the original BlockState's orientation flags. Extension hooks on mod states and `VoxelShape` need the right `BlockGetter` provenance and target position.

Three `MultifaceSpreader.SpreadType` mechanisms require individual acceptance; `SAME_POSITION` is a new property on same cell, `SAME_PLANE` a tangent neighbor, and `WRAP_AROUND` *two local steps and transported target face*. At an edge/corner, the second relative step may run in a different chart from the source: use `PlanetBlockStep` sequentially instead of one global Direction remap, and verify inverse port mapping. If no typed Planet source Level or target chart is authoritative (foreign guidebook Level, structure preview, other dimension), preserve unmodified physical vanilla/mod algorithm.

Vine specifically has **no DOWN property**, relies on UP and world-horizontal cross-plant inheritance and randomTick spreading; treating it as fully six-face multiface will invent invalid states and growth below the source. For source's `UP` on a Planet side, “top” must be a target-local semantic support/movement if and ONLY if this is a Planet-aware vine author; hardcoded Y simulation is otherwise physically meaningful vanilla and foreign-world behavior.

Sculk uses charge-driven and regrow pathways, GlowLichen bone meal, Vine random ticks, and worldgen `MultifaceGrowthFeature`. Each is its own entrypoint (including non-item authors) and can mutate many cells with actual `LevelAccessor.setBlock`, flags, waterlogged fluid tick or post-processing marks. NeoForge loaded-area guards, gamerules, position-aware support and mod `SpreadConfig` callback dispatch MUST survive local gravity integration. Do not intercept a universal `BlockPos.relative` or `Direction.getOpposite` (also used by physical worldgen, redstone, fluids, fake Levels).
