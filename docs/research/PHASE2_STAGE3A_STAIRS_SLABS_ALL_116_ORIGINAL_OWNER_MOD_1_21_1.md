## Algorithm-owner depth: invariants for Planetary

1. [StairBlock](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/level/block/StairBlock.java) owns `getStateForPlacement`: hit FACE + relative click Y yields `HALF`, player horizontal direction yields tangent `FACING`, fluid at physical target yields `WATERLOGGED`, then neighbor geometry determines `SHAPE`. In `getStairsShape`, read source-local FRONT neighbor for OUTER and BACK for INNER, require **equal HALF**, compare tangent directions and check `canTakeShape` with additional side neighbor. `isStairs` uses **instanceof StairBlock**, so ordinary third-party subclasses may join vanilla corner logic automatically; unrelated custom block geometry DOES NOT. The `getShape` built-in 20-shape mapping and `SHAPE_BY_STATE` are canonical local and must rotate exactly once physically via shared `PlanetBlockShapeRuntime`. Do not add global world-Y assumptions.
2. Stair `updateShape` schedules physical water ticks on WATERLOGGED but recomputes corners only when neighbor-update `Direction.getAxis().isHorizontal()`. Under Planetary this physical-horizontal condition is suspect on side/-Y faces. Recompute according to the **source canonical tangent 4-graph**, not absolute world XZ; use `PlanetBlockNeighborQuery` to resolve physical target and target chart, transport **semantic direction**, keep BlockPos physical. The original placement and corner calculation directly call `pos.relative(direction)`, so both need the same local-to-physical neighbor API; patching one callback is insufficient.
3. Stair `rotate/mirror` implementation changes `FACING`, and mirror can update INNER/OUTER left/right; `BlockState` structure/clipboard serialization must use a declared source chart and destination chart (P35/P39), not only rotate models. Base block explosion resistance and properties are material-dependent, not a universal stone/wood constant.
4. [SlabBlock](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/level/block/SlabBlock.java) has independent `getStateForPlacement` and `canBeReplaced` algorithms: same block and same item at target combine into `TYPE=DOUBLE,WATERLOGGED=false`; otherwise clicked canonical `UP/DOWN` and click local half determine TOP/BOTTOM and existing water source state. `canBeReplaced` must reject DOUBLE and different material ITEMs (including modded). Existing [`SlabBlockPlacementMixin`](../../src/main/java/dev/planetary/mixin/SlabBlockPlacementMixin.java) adapts both callbacks for Planet field; that is **an existing adapter, not a passing game test**, and it does not automatically cover subclass overrides.
5. Slab shape is TOP/BOTTOM half or DOUBLE full cube, `useShapeForLightOcclusion` differs for DOUBLE, `getFluidState` projects WATERLOGGED, and `canPlaceLiquid/placeLiquid` reject DOUBLE; `updateShape` schedules water tick. Do not collapse `collision`, `occlusion`, `visual shape`, `raycast`, `support` into one VoxelShape surface: different BlockState lookup paths and Phase3 render face culling can be independent. WATERLOGGED hydrodynamics belong Phase5 but Phase2 must preserve property/state transitions.
6. [WeatheringCopperStairBlock](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/level/block/WeatheringCopperStairBlock.java) and [WeatheringCopperSlabBlock](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/level/block/WeatheringCopperSlabBlock.java) are four each **unwaxed** registered IDs; their override `randomTick -> changeOverTime` and `isRandomlyTicking` are independent of base shapes. Waxed versions are plain StairBlock/SlabBlock in original ZIP, not weathering subclasses. Follow independent `ChangeOverTimeBlock`, `WeatheringCopper`, `HoneycombItem`, `AxeItem`, lightning conversion interaction/registrations before interpreting BLOCK state preservation. A weathering step must retain legal FACING/HALF/SHAPE/WATERLOGGED or TYPE/WATERLOGGED (except intentionally authored transitions). Do not generalize onto every modded copper.
7. Physical `BlockItem` placement, neighbor `updateShape`, structure/feature transformations, commands and fake Level/contraption authors can call separate implementations. Preserve real physical world `Level` and `BlockPos`, and only translate owned local orientation vectors into physical adjacency at source/target. Unknown mod/virtual-world behavior defaults **physical unchanged** unless an explicit typed opt-in or verified registered owner is present.
