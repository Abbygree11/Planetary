## Dispatcher → algorithm owner graph, full branches and pitfalls

1. **Leaves are two different clocks.** `LeavesBlock.updateShape` immediately schedules **1 tick** to recalculate `DISTANCE`; `tick/updateDistance` scans the **six PHYSICAL adjacent** `Direction.values()`, `BlockTags.LOGS` provides distance0 and leaves graph propagates distance up to7. Separate **randomTick** only decays `DISTANCE=7 && !PERSISTENT` (drops then removes). Client `CherryLeavesBlock.animateTick` is merely visual cherry petal and shares inherited server decay. `MangroveLeavesBlock.performBonemeal` produces a separate hanging propagule **physically below**, not part of `LeavesBlock.randomTick`. Every 8 base LeavesBlock material variants and cherry/mangrove subclass must participate; unknown modded leaves may override decay, tags, or distance.
2. **Plant growth/source+target semantic chart.** `StemBlock.randomTick` checks light9 + `CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed`, AGE0..7, then chooses `Direction.Plane.HORIZONTAL` target and checks **physically below target** farmland/dirt; on success writes fruit and *attached_stem* FACING toward fruit in two locations. `AttachedStemBlock.updateShape` watches fruit and reverts to stem, no own randomTick. `CocoaBlock` has four-way FACING attachment to jungle log, AGE0..2 own randomTick, bonemeal separate. `BigDripleafBlock` has four FACING+4 TILT+WATERLOGGED, `entityInside` triggers, scheduled tick tilt UNSTABLE→PARTIAL→FULL→NONE and redstone resets; uses **physical Y height** in `canEntityTilt`, not only floor attachment. `BigDripleafStemBlock` watches both physical UP/DOWN, schedules own tick and WATER fluid tick; bonemeal grows new head above. `PinkPetalsBlock` has FACING+AMOUNT and bonemeal-only multiplication, not a randomTick aging block. Different writers cannot be fixed by a global `Block.randomTick` patch.
3. **Environment/soil.** `SpreadingSnowyDirtBlock.randomTick` is effective owner shared by `GrassBlock` and `MyceliumBlock`: physical `above` snow/light/water and 4 random candidates at X,Z offsets [-1,1], Y [-3,+1], converts target DIRT and sets SNOWY from **target above**. `SnowyDirtBlock` actual `minecraft:podzol` class just watches above for snowy; `GrassBlock.performBonemeal` is a separate **feature** writer. `NyliumBlock` light/above suffocation decay to NETHERRACK plus Nether vegetation configured feature. `FarmBlock` moisture counter 0..7 uses PHYSICAL water scan **X,Z ±4 and Y [0,+1]**, physical rain at `pos.above()`, canSurvive above and entity fallOn trampling; `turnToDirt` calls `pushEntitiesUp` and game event. A universal local `above` remap without classified weather/FluidState provenance silently changes behavior of unknown mods/vanilla `WeatheringCopper` physical-radius4.
4. **No automatic clock in moss and petals.** `MossBlock.performBonemeal` calls configured feature through actual `ChunkGenerator` at `pos.above()`. `PinkPetalsBlock` places/increments count from item and bonemeal, `SnowyDirtBlock` responds to placement/neighbour. Server `ServerLevel.tickChunk` random samples must not be expanded to these registered states merely because they look like aged vegetation.
5. **Interactions and BE simulation.** `RedStoneOreBlock` is activated via attack/stepOn/`useItemOn`, sets `LIT=true`, later **randomTick** `LIT=false`; client animateTick merely emits particles. `SpawnerBlock` delegates tick via `SpawnerBlockEntity.serverTick/clientTick` to `BaseSpawner`, physical blockpos+entity searches/delay NBT. `TrialSpawnerBlock` ticker delegates `TrialSpawnerBlockEntity.getTrialSpawner().tickServer/tickClient`, tracks `TRIAL_SPAWNER_STATE` 6 enum states, `OMINOUS`; accessors write BlockState directly and maintain reward/player tracking. `VaultBlock` has horizontal FACING and `VaultBlockEntity.Server.tick/Client.tick`, own useItemOn and multi-state progression; do not treat BE as `BlockBehaviour.randomTick`, or rotate physical BE storage/loot/NBT accidentally.
