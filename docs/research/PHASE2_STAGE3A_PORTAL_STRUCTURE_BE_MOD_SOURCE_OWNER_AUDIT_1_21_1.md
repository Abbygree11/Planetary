## 1. Portal interface ↔ shape creation ↔ entity dimension transition are distinct authors

- **Nether** `PortalShape.findPortalShape/isValid/createPortalBlocks` scans **physical DOWN and UP**, rectangle rightDir derived from horizontal `Axis.X|Axis.Z`, obsidian/frame predicate, actual structure 2–21 wide and 3–21 high; `NetherPortalBlock.AXIS` original `x,z` only, there is no legal `y` state. - **End gateway** is a `Portal` + `TheEndGatewayBlockEntity`, has BE server `portalTick` and client `beamAnimationTick`, internal cooldown/target location and exit movement.
