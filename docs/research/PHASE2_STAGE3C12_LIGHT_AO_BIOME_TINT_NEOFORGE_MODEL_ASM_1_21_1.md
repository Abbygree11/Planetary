## 1/4 — owner/dispatch graph and exact semantic domains

| Owner | What it owns | Physical vs canonical |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BlockBehaviour.BlockStateBase.getLightEmission(level,pos)`, `getLightBlock(level,pos)`, `getShadeBrightness(level,pos)` | Position-sensitive modded light emission/opacity and shade brightness; static block state cache may hold opaque and light data | `BlockGetter` and physical `BlockPos` are original. State orientation becomes local through Planet opt-in only |
| `LevelLightEngine.getRawBrightness`, sky & block layer engines, auxiliary lights | WORLD XYZ emission, sky falloff/distance and update queues on physical chunks | **PHYSICAL**, not a new local light engine for six gravity faces. Planet local sky/horizon is separate, not automatic local-UP reinterpretation |
| `BlockAndTintGetter.getShade(Direction, boolean)` + NeoForge float-normal variant | Directional side brightness, full vector normal-based shade for mod quad lighter, lightmap source | `Direction` or normal must describe **physical emitted geometry**, not canonical `BlockState.FACING` |
| `BlockRenderDispatcher` | Picks `BakedModel`, gets `ModelData`, routes to `ModelBlockRenderer` or alternate fluid/item/custom renderer | CLIENT model-context owner, not universal for every custom BER/Flywheel |
| `ModelBlockRenderer.tesselateBlock(..., ModelData, RenderType)` | Calls TriState AO policy, dynamic emission, `tesselateWithAO` or `tesselateWithoutAO`, uses quads and `BlockColors` | Source/target physical light samples with physical quad normals, originals retain RenderType/ModelData |
| `ModelBlockRenderer$AmbientOcclusionFace.calculate` | Physical four-edge/corner neighbor brightness, `isViewBlocking`, `getLightBlock`, `getShadeBrightness`, AO remapping from Direction and shape | Physical adjacency and already-rotated normal; vanilla AO corner traversal separate from canonical block state support |
| `LightPipelineAwareModelBlockRenderer` / `SmoothQuadLighter`, `FlatQuadLighter`, `QuadLighter` (version-sensitive) | Experimental NeoForge alternative flat or smooth light pipeline, world `getShade(normalXYZ,shade)`, physical occlusion and per-quad mod color | **Independent override path**; needs exact 21.1.215 presence plus client active-config gating |
| `BlockColors.getColor`, `BlockColor`, `BiomeColors`, `BlockAndTintGetter.getBlockTint` | Per-quad-index tint and arbitrary mod registration; grass, leaves and water sample ambient biome | Biome data is physically keyed by world position. Local block faces still map to correct visual quads; e.g. two-high plants' vanilla `pos.below()` biomes are world-Y-specific |
| `LevelRenderer.getLightColor` and chunk `RenderChunkRegion` | Packed lightmap, physical neighbor levels and region snapshots of ModelData | Avoid replacing render region with active ClientLevel and losing cached tint/ModelData identity |
| `PlanetBakedModelRotation` / `ModelBlockRendererPlanetMixin` | Thin Mixin orients a temporary wrapper over original NeoForge model, transforms geometry+packed normals+quad side, preserves original tint/light metadata | Desired boundary: rotate semantics/normal ONCE; retain mod RenderType, ModelData, Color callback, arbitrary model extension |

## 2/4 — lighting lifecycle, bypasses and risks in current Planet runtime

- Server light updates from Level/Chunk/BlockState light engine and client chunk rebuilds happen independently. Block changes, random ticks, worldgen direct ChunkAccess writes, structure placement and dynamically-changing mod BE ModelData can alter emitted light and tint without any `ModelBlockRendererPlanetMixin` call on server. NeoForge model-data snapshots and rebuild invalidation must be preserved.
- AO lightmap and brightness use actual *physical* X/Y/Z neighbor positions. **Local UP gravity does not imply local sky light origin.** Altering the global `Direction.UP` or `LightLayer.SKY` semantics would break vanilla world shadows, mod light engines and foreign fake worlds. If future Planet worlds implement a separate local-sky model, they need explicit data/level-owner integration, not a rendering-side magic Y remap.
- Vanilla `BlockColors.createDefault` uses grass/foliage/water biome resolutions; for tall grass/LARGE_FERN, the UPPER half chooses `pos.below()` to sample the lower half's biome. On a rotated Planet gravity face that position is not automatically local DOWN; need a typed source block/chart adjacency rule if biomes genuinely vary. However do not universally alter client BlockColor callbacks because mods may intentionally sample physical pos.
- Current `PlanetBakedModelRotation` wraps original models and preserves tint index and quads normals via NeoForge transforms. **This alone does not prove AO/smooth-light correctness:** `ModelBlockRenderer` and possible `LightPipelineAwareModelBlockRenderer` own separate per-neighbor physical sampling and mod hooks.
- Current Planet shader/renders custom BER, liquids, accelerated Flywheel/CTM and third-party shader pipelines are separate. `PlanetClientRenderFrame.stateFrameAt` fallback from non-Level BlockGetter to active ClientLevel can potentially attach foreign fake render worlds incorrectly (Stage11), and stale `PlanetBlockRenderCulling` cache of dynamic mod occlusion is a separate Stage11 dependency.
- Existing `PlanetBakedModelRotation.orientForBlock` specifically detects `SpreadingSnowyDirtBlock` when selecting multi-chart seam treatment; arbitrary mod grass/canopy blocks using different inheritance may not share this policy. Do NOT solve by enumerating block IDs; future Phase3 source+target chart/tint callback adapter requires typed provenance and representative loaded-mod tests.
- User has reported visually odd shadows on side faces. This symptom can originate from rotated quad normals, sampled physical AO/light, sky engine assumptions, mod render cache, or an entirely different dynamic shader path. Source/ABI research **cannot establish a root cause without in-game capture**.

## 3/4 — deepest safe common mechanism / arbitrary mods

| Classification | Owned mechanism | Required rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **PHYSICAL_DO_NOT_TOUCH** | World LevelLightEngine, physical chunk skylight & block light, BlockAndTintGetter positional sampling, actual lightmap, real XYZ neighbor positions, LightLayer.SKY | Vanilla/no-Planet world and mod light providers cannot accept chart-local rewrites globally |
| **SHARED_SAFE** | NeoForge TriState model AO, ModelData/RenderType and `BakedModelWrapper`; mod BlockColor registered callbacks, dynamic State getLightEmission/getLightBlock, transparent/dynamic models | Preserve original callbacks and their data; transform emitted quad representation at semantic boundary only |
| **OPT_IN_ALGORITHM** | Planet-owned source/target frames, rotated quad normals, optional typed `PlanetLightQueryContext` (position, physical model side, canonical chart, shade normal, render getter provenance) and explicit seam/light sampling when algorithm really expects local UP | Only for verified Planet-origin positions/regions and designated semantic callbacks, never global `getShade`/LightLayer mutation |
| **MOD_INDEPENDENT** | NeoForge experimental light-pipeline path, external light shaders/CTM, custom BER/accelerated Create/Flywheel/BE light, independent mod biome textures and color resolvers | Classify actual owner; test loaded mod JAR and original callbacks; do not assume subclass of vanilla block/renderer |
| **CLIENT_RENDER** | AO corner weights, flat light, coloring and shaders, tint/biome lookup, normals, breaking overlay | Phase3 code & screenshot/client/real-mod performance acceptance |

**Why one owner deeper unsafe:** modifying `LevelLightEngine`, `LightLayer`, `BlockPos`, `BlockAndTintGetter.getShade` or any general `BlockColor` would change physical light semantics in every dimension, render region, foreign contraption Level and third-party shader. **Why one named layer shallower insufficient:** flipping side-face grass/stone/leaf quads alone misses AO neighbor sampling, NeoForge alternative QuadLighter, mod dynamic lights/tint and ModelData. Stable Planet-owned typed provenance + versioned thin renderer entry adapter gives broadest safe mod compatibility.
