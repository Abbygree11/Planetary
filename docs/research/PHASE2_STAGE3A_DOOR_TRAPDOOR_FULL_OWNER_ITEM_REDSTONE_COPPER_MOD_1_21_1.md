## Owner-depth: original actual placement, block update and restrictions

### Real door placement source chain (all materials)
[BlockItem](https://github.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/blob/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/item/BlockItem.java) is the common item dispatcher; [DoubleHighBlockItem](https://github.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/blob/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/item/DoubleHighBlockItem.java) **overrides `placeBlock`**: obtains real `clickedPos.above()`, gets real `isWaterAt(upperPos)`, first `level.setBlock(upperPos,WATER or AIR,27)`, and only THEN `super.placeBlock(context,state)`. This is a user-visible *preclear of physical upper block*; do not confuse with DoorBlock own second-half write, and do not globally rewrite BlockItem/Level since other DoubleHigh users and modded Items share these paths. [DoorBlock](https://github.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/blob/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/level/block/DoorBlock.java) `getStateForPlacement` checks *physical world* max Y and `clickedPos.above()` canBeReplaced, then chooses horizontal `FACING`, nearby geometry-derived `HINGE`, and power from **both lower and upper** real positions; `setPlacedBy` writes `UPPER` `pos.above()` flags3, distinct from preceding DoubleHighBlockItem preclear. Door `updateShape` verifies matching opposite half along PHYSICAL Y, may take copied state from `instanceof DoorBlock`, handles lost supports and removes invalid half; `canSurvive` on LOWER tests below block `isFaceSturdy(...,Direction.UP)`, UPPER checks physical lower. `neighborChanged` tests redstone on **both parts**, updates POWERED/OPEN, playSound/GameEvent as appropriate. `getHinge` reads both lateral and diagonal real blocks and click point; `useWithoutItem` can toggle only permitted `BlockSetType`. `playerWillDestroy` special other-half cleanup and loot. `onExplosionHit` WindCharge permission comes from BlockSetType, not enum material string. AI door access and block pathfinding belong to distinct callsites.

### Trapdoor chain is NOT two-block
[TrapDoorBlock](https://github.com/hackersense/OptiFine-Source/blob/b77c5c6995874f6cf2755bc5234428906b337b75/1.21.1/net/minecraft/world/level/block/TrapDoorBlock.java) extends **HorizontalDirectionalBlock** and implements **SimpleWaterloggedBlock**. Physical `BlockItem` places exactly ONE block; `getStateForPlacement` uses clicked physical face and click y threshold (or horizontal player direction) to select FACING and HALF.TOP/BOTTOM, plus fluid = source `Fluids.WATER` to store WATERLOGGED. `canSurvive` and `setPlacedBy` are inherited BlockBehaviour/Block, not DoorBlock; `updateShape` schedules actual `Fluids.WATER` tick, `neighborChanged` takes its own redstone power at one position and may schedule water tick, `useWithoutItem` toggles only if type.canOpenByHand. **Do not** build arbitrary second block for modded trapdoor. Side-face/ceiling positioning requires local semantic normal and tangent transform but legal vanilla FACING remains horizontal on the physical state.
