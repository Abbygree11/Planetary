## 2/4 — complete independent lifecycle, local chart, bypasses and order

1. **Initial BlockItem/placement vs direct code writer.** `GrowingPlantBlock.getStateForPlacement(BlockPlaceContext)` probes **`clickedPos.relative(growthDirection)`**; if existing head or body there, writes body state, else invokes `getStateForPlacement(LevelAccessor)` of head or body. Head initializes AGE to random 0..24. Kelp placement has liquid validity: **don't turn non-water into water by rewriting one BlockState**.
2. **Support/survival.** `canSurvive` uses **source `pos.relative(growthDirection.getOpposite())`** and `blockstate.isFaceSturdy(level,supportPos,growthDirection)` OR matching head/body. It is an *opposite-port* check; at cross-face seams “above below” must be interpreted relative to a specific source and target chart, not a global physical Y rewrite. Scheduled `tick` removes unsupported cells, which can cascade chain fragmentation.
3. **Head growth random tick.** AGE < 25 / chance, mod `CropGrowEvent.Pre`, target `pos.relative(growthDirection)`, polymorphic `canGrowInto`, `getGrowIntoState` (AGE increment + mod/vines overrides), `ServerLevel.setBlockAndUpdate`, `CropGrowEvent.Post` only after write. New cell is **head**. Neighborhood update converts old head to **body** through `updateShape`; cave vine passes BERRIES onto old body.
4. **Body head conversion.** If neighbor at **`growthDirection`** ceases to be body/head, body `updateShape` calls `updateHeadAfterConvertedFromBody(oldBodyState, head.getStateForPlacement(level))`: creates legal new head at remaining endpoint, with new AGE + inherited special properties (e.g. cave-vines BERRIES). If support at `growthDirection.getOpposite()` fails, schedule physical tick to remove cell. `scheduleFluidTicks` owner preserves water schedule for kelp; avoid double tick or lost fluid updates.
5. **Bonemeal on head vs body.** Head `performBonemeal` loops variable count via `getBlocksToGrowWhenBonemealed(RandomSource)`, repeatedly writes new AGE head and steps `relative(growthDirection)`; body locates **real end head** via `BlockUtil.getTopConnectedBlock(LevelGetter,pos,currentBlock,growthDirection,headBlock)`, then forwards to that head's *virtual* `performBonemeal`. Mod overrides may completely change behavior, including bonemeal-at-cell rather than chain extension for CaveVines BERRIES.
6. **Other authors / worldgen.** `KelpFeature.place` writes direct head/body `BlockState` as a physical vertical column; `TwistingVinesFeature` and `WeepingVinesFeature` likewise create a fixed direction chain with direct `LevelAccessor.setBlock`, with world Y build limits and specific environment predicates. They do **not** go through player placement, so item-only patches are insufficient. Schematic template, commands and mod worldgen likewise can place already-authored state, and mods may override virtual field predicates on the base classes.

### Physical and Planet chart requirements (tests NOT executed)

Let `source = (actual Level, physical BlockPos, chart, localGrowthDir, author)`. At a seam, `growthDirection` is a *vanilla world-physical field stored in block owner*; **transport each chain step into a target chart using `PlanetBlockFrameContext.step(localDirection)`**, produce the real physical target BlockPos and transported target-local orientation before probing `canGrowInto` and writing. Never permanently mutate global `Direction.UP/DOWN`, `BlockPos.relative` or Level; multiple head/body cells in an extended cross-seam chain can have distinct faces/charts. `BlockUtil.getTopConnectedBlock` currently uses a fixed physical Direction; a local re-implementation **only for Planet-owned chain lookup** must preserve bounded termination and mod virtual APIs. GrowthDirection is a class field, not a vanilla block state property: a naive stored BlockState rotate is insufficient.

**Invariant:** two-way chart reciprocity under seam traversal (normal and inverted gravity), physically different cells only one actual `Level.setBlock` author per cell, no duplicate event/tick. A broken support triggers identical NeoForge event and survival semantics as vanilla but target chart valid. If input is foreign Level/guidebook/structure preview, preserve original behavior **without consulting `Minecraft.getInstance().level`**. Worldgen and BlockUtil remain physical for worlds without a resolved Planet source/target frame; DO NOT intercept global physical BlockPos or directional checks.
