## Important source algorithm distinctions

1. **16 glaze colors** share `GlazedTerracottaBlock.getStateForPlacement = context.getHorizontalDirection().getOpposite()`, legal horizontal `FACING`. `HorizontalDirectionalBlock` separately owns generic rotate/mirror (no universal placement). Motif is authored in **blockstate model selection and baked UV/face quads**; block constructor **does not rotate texture pixels**. For a 2×2 motif, rotate **each model relative to the canonical chart exactly once**, and verify adjacent correct quadrants, not merely the center block looking right. Unglazed terracotta has no required `FACING`: it may have a visible static material texture but is NOT this same state owner. Model assets not present in Planetary repo; vanilla exact baked/asset rendering remains pending until resource-pack/runtime audit.
2. **Pumpkin** `PumpkinBlock` owns NO FACING state. A direct **SHEARS** `useItemOn` author converts it to `minecraft:carved_pumpkin`, picks front direction from clicked face or player direction on vertical hit, writes state via `Level.setBlock(...,11)` and physically spawns four seeds. It does NOT call carved `BlockItem.getStateForPlacement`. Separately `CarvedPumpkinBlock` is the direct placement owner for `minecraft:jack_o_lantern`, and `EquipableCarvedPumpkinBlock` inherits its placement owner for `minecraft:carved_pumpkin`. `CarvedPumpkinBlock.onPlace` triggers iron/snow golem `BlockPattern` scanning and new entity spawn; golem pattern generation/physical geometry is separate Phase7/8. Treat SHEARS, golems, loot, item-as-helmet and rendering as distinct calls.
3. **Loom** is a simple horizontal model face/texture and menu owner, not a multi-direction CTM algorithm. **Anvil** three wear stages own FACING`getHorizontalDirection().getClockWise()` (different from glaze!), asymmetric voxel shape, FallingBlock.updateShape, falling damage/wear block-identity transitions preserving FACING. Render/physics are separate Phase3 and Phase7 gates.
4. **EndPortalFrame** owns eye+facing state, structure-sensitive portal connectivity and Eye of Ender item interaction; do not rotate portal algorithms blindly, route Phase9 portal-owner contract. **Jigsaw** owns exactly 12 legal `FrontAndTop` orientations, chooses top independently from clicked front, has admin `GameMasterBlockItem` rather than generic BlockItem, BE and `JigsawBlock.canAttach` matching in structure coordinates. **CommandBlock** owns six-direction `FACING` and `CONDITIONAL`, three variants use admin `GameMasterBlockItem`, `setPlacedBy` owns BE metadata and command chain processing follows its own directional algorithm, Phase7A. No admin/permissions bypass is appropriate.
5. Pinned vanilla comparative placement semantics are **not** evidence that a Planet thin versioned adapter exists or is hooked. Existing `PlanetBlockStateFrame` (canonical source/target), `PlanetBakedModelRotation` and `ModelBlockRendererPlanetMixin.tesselateBlock` rotate static baked quads; it is a *candidate renderer for orientation-correct static block assets*, not proof of modded JSON/CTM/BER and server-state placement correctness. Texture `uvlock`, model JSON `x/y` rotations, vanilla `BlockState` rotate/mirror and Planet gravity frame must remain **distinct transforms** and must not double-rotate one another.
